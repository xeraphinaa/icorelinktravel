Booking Guidelines & Agency Policies

Important details to know before confirming your travel packages

Booking Guidelines
1. Browse and select a travel package. 2. Fill out the booking details including traveler information. 3. Upload a valid government-issued ID for verification. 4. Pay the Reservation Fee of Php 2,000 per person or choose Full Payment to secure your slot. 5. Upload proof of payment via the customer dashboard. 6. Wait for verification (12-24 hours). You will receive an email and notification upon confirmation.
Cancellation Policy
• Cancellations made more than 15 days before the travel date are subject to a standard cancellation fee of 15% of the total amount. • Cancellations made 7 to 14 days before the travel date are subject to a 50% cancellation fee. • Cancellations made less than 7 days prior to travel are non-refundable. • No-shows on the travel date are billed 100% and cannot be refunded or rebooked.
Refund Policy
• Reservation fees are non-refundable but transferable to another package or person under specific terms. • Approved refunds will be processed within 15-30 business days via bank transfer or GCash. • Service fees incurred during payment processing (e.g., PayPal fees) are non-refundable.
